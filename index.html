Discovery best practices

  • Ask one at a time. Let the learner fully answer before moving on.
  • Listen and validate. Reflect their answer back with follow-up questions.
  • Explore depth. Technical depth, challenges, motivations and career goals.
  • Personalize the value. Connect their pain points to the right program.
  • For AI learners. Probe current AI exposure, tools, frameworks and projects.
  • For job change / interview prep. Interview history, target companies, comp goals and challenges.

Our goal: Uncover the real motivation, identify the right learning path, and help every learner achieve their career goals.
You are the bridge to their success!
